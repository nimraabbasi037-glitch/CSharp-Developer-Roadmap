# Comparison Operators in C#

## Definition

Comparison operators are used to compare two values. The result is always **True** or **False**.

## Why do we use Comparison Operators?

Comparison operators are used to check conditions in a program. They are mostly used with **if-else statements**, **loops**, and decision-making.

## Comparison Operators

| Operator | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| == | Equal to |
| != | Not Equal to |
| > | Greater Than |
| < | Less Than |
| >= | Greater Than or Equal to |
| <= | Less Than or Equal to |

## Examples

10 == 10 → True

10 != 5 → True

10 > 5 → True

10 < 5 → False

10 >= 10 → True

5 <= 10 → True

## Key Points

- Comparison operators compare two values.
- The result is always **True** or **False**.
- They are commonly used with **if-else** statements.
